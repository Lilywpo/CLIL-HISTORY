Language focus: the past simple

Grammar & Verb Hunt

📌 Remember: Past Simple Rules

• Regular verbs: add -ed or -d: plant → planted, live → lived, carry → carried, stop → stopped

• Irregular verbs: learn them! grow → grew, make → made, keep → kept

• Negative: did not + base verb: They did not stop hunting.

• Question: What did they eat?
